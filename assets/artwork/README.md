# Instrument artwork

`instrument-atlas.png` is an original AI-generated instrument illustration atlas,
created for QuadraJ's Reverie Ensemble using the built-in OpenAI image generation
tool on 2026-10-24. It is illustrative catalog art, not photographs of the exact
instruments that were sampled. No DAW assets were used as references.

4 × 3 tiles: grand, upright, acoustic guitar, hollowbody electric guitar;
bass, violin, drums, synthesizer; flute, trumpet, harp, marimba.
Violin is explicitly a family fallback for other bowed strings; this is not
an instrument-specific cello picture. Unknown instruments use synthesizer fallback.

QuadraJ dedicates its rights in this generated asset under CC0-1.0; rights in
generated output may vary by jurisdiction. Source: OpenAI image generation.

three rows, warm paper #E8E1D5 background, gentle graphite/sepia grading, complete
instruments with margins, physically accurate structure, no labels, logos,

`orchestra-atlas.png` was generated with the same tool, date and rights dedication.
4 × 4 tiles: cello, double bass, viola, bassoon; clarinet, oboe, French horn,
trombone; tuba, piccolo, organ, vibraphone; kalimba, balafon, timpani, tubular bells.
Prompt: one precise square 4×4 atlas of the sixteen named instruments, realistic
studio catalog materials and accurate structures, fully visible, equal tiles,
warm paper #E8E1D5, restrained sepia/graphite, no text, logos or people.
These are generated illustrations, not sample-session photographs.

`icons/*.svg` are original QuadraJ vector silhouettes for small track headers.
They are Apache-2.0, retain distinguishable families and render with Qt SVG.
Large artwork always comes from the raster atlas or supplied image URI.
Missing SVG support falls back to the cached family image, never a large
procedural drawing.
